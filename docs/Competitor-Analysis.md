# Competitor Analysis → what Food Raksha should borrow

Analysed July 2026: CliniExperts, Vakilsearch, Cleartax (Indian) · SGS, Intertek, Registrar Corp, FoodChainID (global). Below: the patterns worth stealing, mapped to our build.

---

## The two playbooks — and our sweet spot

**Global certifiers (SGS, Intertek, Registrar Corp, FoodChainID)** — premium restraint. Cinematic hero, real photography, one memorable promise, huge whitespace, few words.

**Indian mass-market (Vakilsearch, Cleartax, CliniExperts)** — conversion machines. SEO-optimised H1s, sticky lead forms above the fold, WhatsApp, dense service catalogs, aggressive trust badges.

> **Food Raksha's edge = Apple restraint in the LAYOUT + Indian conversion mechanics in the FUNNEL.** Clean and premium like Registrar Corp, but with Vakilsearch's sticky lead form, WhatsApp, pricing and trust density. Nobody in the Indian FSSAI space looks premium *and* converts hard. That gap is ours.

---

## 8 things to borrow (each mapped to a page)

### 1. One outcome-emotional promise, not a feature list
Every global leads with certainty: SGS *"When you need to be sure"* · Intertek *"Total Quality. Assured."* · FoodChainID *"Your trusted food… partner"* · Registrar *"One Partner for All Your Compliance Needs."*
→ **Home hero.** Our "Your food licence, without the headache" is good but leans convenience. Add a certainty layer — e.g. *"FSSAI licensing you can be sure about."* Test both.

### 2. Two-tone headline with one accent word
Registrar Corp: navy headline, **blue** accent word. The old Food Raksha site did green+blue. Our prototype already does this — **validated, keep it**, but Apple-restrained (one accent word, not a rainbow).

### 3. Dual CTA — lead-capture primary + self-serve secondary
Registrar Corp: **"Talk to an Expert"** (filled) + **"Register with FDA"** (outline). This is exactly our model.
→ **Every page.** Primary green/blue pill = "Book free consultation" (→ Lead). Secondary ghost = "Start application" (→ signup). Already in our prototype.

### 4. Sticky, above-the-fold, 3-field lead form  ← the highest-impact borrow
Vakilsearch's FSSAI page: a pinned card — **Email · Mobile · City/Pincode · WhatsApp toggle · "Get Started."** Three fields, always visible while you scroll the SEO copy.
→ **Services + all licence pages.** This is the single most effective pattern in the Indian set. Our qualifier should become a **sticky sidebar form** on content pages, not just a hero block.

### 5. The trust stack — stack ALL of these, they compound
- **Trustpilot / Google star rating** up top (Registrar Corp puts 5 stars above the H1).
- **Named client logos** of recognised brands (Registrar: Unilever, Hermès). Food Raksha already has Karim's, Marine Lifesciences, Dr Agarwals — feature them prominently.
- **Concrete numbers** ("35,000+ companies" — Registrar; "120+ experts" — Food Raksha's own Book page).
- **ISO badge** ("Only ISO 27001 Certified Platform" — Vakilsearch's trust chip).
- **Named testimonials** with person + title + company (CliniExperts, Vakilsearch) — far stronger than anonymous quotes.
- **Media features** (CliniExperts: Forbes, India Today, ET logos). If Food Raksha has any press, show it.
→ **Home + every service page.** We currently have a thin logo strip; build the full stack.

### 6. An SEO content engine — this is how they actually get traffic
Cleartax's FSSAI page *is* an SEO article (author byline, "Updated Apr 2025", "7 min read"). CliniExperts runs a **regulatory-updates hub** (dated FSSAI/CDSCO updates — a freshness + authority signal). This is where Indian FSSAI demand is won.
→ **Explore More → Blog + FSSAI Updates.** Build the blog as real, indexable articles with author/date/read-time, plus a dated "FSSAI Updates" feed. Architect the site for this from Stage 1 (we already flagged programmatic SEO).

### 7. India-specific conversion accelerators
- **WhatsApp opt-in** on every form (Vakilsearch, and Food Raksha's own Book page already has it) — huge in India.
- **Hindi toggle** (Vakilsearch: "Now read this page in Hindi"). Worth offering on the marketing site even though the CRM is English-only — it lifts trust and conversion with smaller FBOs.

### 8. Warm, real photography
FoodChainID (food), SGS (lab scientists), Cleartax (a smiling expert with floating trust chips: "24hr filing guarantee"). Real people/food humanise a compliance purchase.
→ **Hero + trust sections.** Use warm food-business photography (a dhaba owner, a bakery, a packaging line), and float small trust chips on the hero image like Cleartax.

## What NOT to copy

- **CliniExperts' / Vakilsearch's mega-catalog density** — hundreds of links, overwhelming. Our Apple-clean IA is a differentiator; keep it lean.
- **SGS/Intertek's enterprise coldness** — beautiful but aloof; our buyer is a small FBO who needs reassurance, not gravitas.
- **Cleartax's article-only landing** — great for SEO, but the page is a wall of text with weak conversion; we pair SEO copy *with* the sticky form.

---
