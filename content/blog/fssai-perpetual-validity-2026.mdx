For years, the calendar reminder every food business dreaded was the FSSAI renewal. Miss it, and you risked penalties or a lapse in your licence. The 2026 reform changes that.

## Licences are now perpetual

Since the reform, FSSAI registrations and licences **do not expire**. Once you're licensed, you stay licensed — no renewal cycle, no expiry date to track.

## What this means in practice

- **No renewal fees or paperwork** on an ongoing basis.
- **No lapse risk** from a missed deadline.
- Your compliance focus shifts from *renewing* to *keeping your details current* — you must still notify FSSAI of any material change to your business, like a new address, category or capacity.

## What about older licences?

Licences issued **before** the reform still carry their original expiry until they're migrated. If you hold a legacy licence with an expiry date, it's worth confirming its status so nothing slips through.

## Annual returns still apply

Perpetual validity doesn't remove every obligation. Certain businesses — manufacturers and importers in particular — still file annual returns. Perpetual validity is about the *licence*, not about going compliance-free.
