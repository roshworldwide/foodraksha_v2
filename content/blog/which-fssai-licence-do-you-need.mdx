Every food business in India needs an FSSAI licence or registration to operate legally. Which one you need comes down to a single number: your **annual turnover**.

## The three tiers

There are three levels, and the thresholds are set by law:

- **Basic Registration** — turnover **up to ₹1.5 crore**. The entry level, for the smallest businesses.
- **State Licence** — turnover **₹1.5 crore to ₹50 crore**. For mid-sized businesses operating within one state.
- **Central Licence** — turnover **above ₹50 crore**, plus all importers, exporters and businesses operating across multiple states or at ports and airports.

If you sit exactly on a boundary, ₹1.5 crore is still Basic and ₹50 crore is still State.

## What changed in 2026

Two things are worth knowing after the reform:

1. **Registrations and licences are now perpetual.** There are no renewals — once issued, your licence stays valid. Only legacy licences from before the reform still carry an expiry.
2. **Basic Registration under ₹1.5 crore is instant.** There's no pre-inspection for the smallest businesses, so you can be up and running in days.

## Not sure where you land?

If your turnover is close to a threshold, or you import, export, or run in more than one state, it's worth a quick check before you file — the wrong application means a rejection and a restart.
