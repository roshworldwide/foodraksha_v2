A State Licence is for mid-sized food businesses — turnover between **₹1.5 crore and ₹50 crore**, operating within a single state. The application is more involved than a Basic Registration, so getting the documents right the first time matters.

## The core documents

Almost every State Licence application needs:

- **Form B**, completed and signed (we prepare this from your answers).
- **Photo ID and address proof** of the proprietor, partners or directors.
- A **premises layout plan** showing the area and equipment.
- A **list of directors, partners or the proprietor** with contact details.
- A **NOC from the premises owner** where the premises are rented.

## Extra documents by business type

Depending on what you do, you may also need:

- A **water test report** from a recognised lab — required for manufacturers.
- A **food safety management (FSMS) plan** or declaration.
- Proof of turnover, or the relevant incorporation documents.
