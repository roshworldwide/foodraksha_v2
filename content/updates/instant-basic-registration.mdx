Small food businesses can now get their FSSAI **Basic Registration instantly**. For applicants with annual turnover up to **₹1.5 crore**, there is no pre-inspection step — the registration is issued on a valid application.

## Who this affects

This covers the smallest food businesses: home kitchens, home bakers, petty retailers, hawkers, tea stalls and small traders. If your turnover is under ₹1.5 crore, you're on the Basic Registration track.

## What you still need

Instant doesn't mean no paperwork. You'll still provide a photo ID, a passport-size photo, proof of your business address, and a declaration of what your business does. Get those right and the registration follows immediately.
