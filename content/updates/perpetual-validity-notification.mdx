FSSAI registrations and licences now carry **perpetual validity**. The renewal cycle has been discontinued: once your licence is issued, it stays valid.

## Key points

- **No renewals** for registrations and licences issued under the new regime.
- **Legacy licences** issued before the change keep their existing expiry until migrated.
- Businesses must still **keep their details current** and file any applicable **annual returns**.

## What to do now

If you hold an older licence with an expiry date, confirm whether it has been migrated to perpetual validity. If you're applying fresh, your new licence will be perpetual from the start.
